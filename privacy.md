# Home Glass — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 10, 2026_

Home Glass is an app for Apple Vision Pro, iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV that shows and controls your own smart home.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** Home Glass has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own. We never receive your data.

## What stays on your devices
- **Apple Home (HomeKit):** read and controlled on the device through Apple's HomeKit framework, with your permission.
- **Home Assistant:** the app talks directly to *your* Home Assistant server. Its address is stored on the device; your access key is stored in the device Keychain. When you send this connection to an Apple TV, it goes directly from your device to the Apple TV over your home network.
- **Your layout and settings** (room order, names, icons, hidden devices, groups, widgets) are stored on the device.

## iCloud
If iCloud is turned on for Home Glass, your layout and settings are kept in sync between your own devices through your private iCloud storage, run by Apple. We can't see it.

## Weather
To show the local forecast, Home Glass uses your approximate location, only with your permission, or the city you choose. The forecast comes from Apple Weather; if it isn't available, your approximate coordinates are sent to the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway) to get the forecast.

## Connections you choose to set up
Only if you set them up, Home Glass talks directly to these services or devices from your own device, with your own account, key or token. Keys and tokens are stored in the device Keychain. Nothing goes through us.
- **Directly on your home network:** Philips Hue bridge, Shelly devices, IKEA DIRIGERA hub, Somfy TaHoma box.
- **Through the manufacturer's official service:** Samsung SmartThings, SwitchBot, Tuya · Smart Life, Aqara, Govee, LG ThinQ, Nuki, Home Connect (Bosch, Siemens, Neff and others).
- **Apple Music** (Apple's MusicKit) and **Spotify** to play your music on your speakers.
- **OpenAI** (image generation) to illustrate a device: the description and the photo you choose are sent to OpenAI with *your* API key. Nothing is sent unless you ask for an illustration.

Each service's own privacy policy applies to what it receives from your device.

## Purchases
In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details.

## Children
Home Glass is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its date.
