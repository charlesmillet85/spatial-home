# Spatial Home — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 28, 2026_

Spatial Home is an app for Apple Vision Pro that shows and controls your own smart home.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** Spatial Home has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own. We never receive your data.

## What stays on your Vision Pro
- **Apple Home (HomeKit):** read and controlled on the device through Apple's HomeKit framework, with your permission.
- **Home Assistant:** the app talks directly to *your* Home Assistant server on your network. Its address is stored on the device; your access key is stored in the device Keychain.
- **Your layout and settings** (room order, names, hidden devices, widgets) are stored on the device.

## Optional services you choose to connect
Only if you turn them on, Spatial Home talks directly to them from your device, with your own account or key:
- **Apple Music** (Apple's MusicKit) to play your playlists.
- **Spotify** (Spotify Web API) to show your playlists and play them on your speakers.
- **OpenAI** (image generation) to illustrate a device: the description and the photo you choose are sent to OpenAI with *your* API key. Nothing is sent unless you ask for an illustration.
Their own privacy policies apply to what you send them.

## Purchases
The one-time Pro purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details.

## Children
Spatial Home is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.
