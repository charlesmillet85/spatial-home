# Spatial Home

Your smart home, in your space, on Apple Vision Pro. Spatial Home works with Apple Home (HomeKit) and Home Assistant.

## Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email **[charlesmillet@me.com](mailto:charlesmillet@me.com)**. We usually answer within two days.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I connect Home Assistant?**
In Settings › Home Assistant, open the *Connection guide*. Spatial Home finds your server on the network; you then create a long-lived access token in your Home Assistant profile (Security tab) and paste it. The token stays in your Vision Pro's Keychain.

**Can I try it without a smart home?**
Yes. On the welcome screen, choose *Try the demo*.

**How do I put widgets on my walls?**
Look at a wall, open the Widgets app, pick a Spatial Home widget and place it. Settings › Wall widgets shows every step.

**What's free, and what does Pro include?**
Free: your Home page and your first 2 rooms (reorder rooms to choose which), music on 2 speakers with Apple Music, and 2 wall widgets (Switch and Room lights).
Pro, a one-time purchase with no subscription, opens every room, every speaker and group with Sonos favorites, radio and Spotify, every wall widget (Climate, Camera, Music, TV, Lock, Home), live cameras and AI illustrations. You can restore it from Settings.
